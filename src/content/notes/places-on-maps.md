I was tutoring an eighth grader when I was reminded of how much I used to love History and Geography.

I spent so much time playing Geo Challenge when no one was interested in buying me on Friends for Sale. 🤣 I even won a schoolwide “Name that Flag” quiz bee. Memorizing blank maps and country and province capitals wasn’t a chore when our Hekasi/AP teacher required it.

I think I’ve wanted to see the world since my dad brought home a globe. I wanted to experience the colorful places I’d only seen in black and white in textbooks.

In an old journal entry, I wrote that I hoped every daily task accomplished was a small step toward setting foot in places I could only pin on maps.

I moved from the Philippines to Spain in 2026. These days I’m building a life in Salamanca.

Spain is a chapter, though. Home is still part of the story.
